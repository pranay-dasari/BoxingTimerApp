AUDIO FILES NEEDED:

Place these 3 audio files in this folder:

1. single_bell.mp3 - Single bell sound (for buffer start and rest end)
2. double_bell.mp3 - Double bell sound (for training end)  
3. final_bell.mp3 - Final bell sound (for workout completion)

The app will work without these files, but no sound will play.

Download bell sounds from:
- freesound.org (search "boxing bell")
- zapsplat.com (free with registration)
- Any short bell sound (1-3 seconds each)

Make sure files are named exactly as listed above!
